# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We welcome participation from people of every background and commit to a respectful, harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence in good faith.
- Give actionable, respectful feedback.
- Protect private, sensitive, and proprietary information.
- Respect differing experience levels and perspectives.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, or deliberately unsafe instructions are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report security concerns through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting; report conduct concerns through a private maintainer contact before opening a public issue.
